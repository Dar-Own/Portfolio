# Prototype AMM adapters violate withdrawal and balance interfaces

## Description

Integrator configures a prototype strategy → Vault relies on deposit returning post-deposit balance and withdraw honoring amount/recipient while returning remaining balance → both adapters return incorrect balances and ignore withdrawal amount/recipient; Xycloans directs proceeds to Vault and uses an authorization destination inconsistent with funding, while Soroswap reports its router as the asset → investments fail or accounting and delivery diverge. Successful share burn without the quoted strategy-funded payout requires partial plumbing repair.

Source interface violations and funding authorization failure proved. Full unwind/unpaid quoted withdrawal after partial plumbing repair is conditional; live affected vault and outsider profit not demonstrated.

### Mechanism step by step

#### Step 1 — The interface requires amount and recipient fidelity

The vault depends on post-operation strategy balance returns and delivery of the requested underlying to the specified recipient.

#### Step 2 — The adapter ignores the requested amount and recipient

The adapter exits its entire position, routes swap output to from rather than to, and returns swap output rather than remaining strategy balance.

Source: `source/[project]-1.0.0/apps/contracts/strategies/xycloans/src/lib.rs:96-119`.

        from.require_auth();

        let xycloans_address = get_xycloans_pool_address(&e);
        let xycloans_pool_client = XycloansPoolClient::new(&e, &xycloans_address);

        let shares: i128 = xycloans_pool_client.shares(&from);
        xycloans_pool_client.withdraw(&from, &shares);

        let matured: i128 = xycloans_pool_client.matured(&from);
        xycloans_pool_client.withdraw_matured(&from);

        let total: i128 = shares.checked_add(matured).unwrap();

        let pool_token = get_pool_token(&e);
        let token_in = get_token_in(&e);

        let result = swap(&e, &from, &pool_token, &token_in, &total);

        Ok(result)
    }

## Proof of concept

The authorization harness uses test-only VaultLike and StrategyLike contracts with synthetically minted tokens. Its negative test rejects a vault-to-pair transfer when authorization covers only vault-to-strategy; its control succeeds when destinations match. Two passing tests and successful compilation were historically reported. This verifies the existing authorization failure, not an end-to-end withdrawal loss. The unpaid-withdrawal consequence requires partially repaired plumbing, and no affected deployed adapter was found.

**Proof limits:** Executable evidence proves an authorization failure and its control; hypothetical post-repair user loss is source-derived.

fn vault_to_strategy_authorization_does_not_cover_vault_to_pair() {
